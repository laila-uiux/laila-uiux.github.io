A little tour of the Frutiger Aero look

Frutiger Aero Timeline

Named after the Frutiger typeface and Windows Aero.

  • 2001 Apple's Aqua look gives buttons a glossy, water-drop shine.
  • 2004 The glossy style starts replacing the Y2K look.
  • 2007 Windows Vista and the first iPhone.
  • 2009 Windows 7 makes the glassy Aero look everywhere.
  • 2013 Flat design, like iOS 7, takes over.
  • 2017 The name "Frutiger Aero" is coined.
  • Today Nostalgia brings it back.

Things you'll see

Bubbles, water droplets, dolphins and fish, blue skies, green grass, and glossy glass buttons.
