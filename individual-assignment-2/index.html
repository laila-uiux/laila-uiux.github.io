Tea, Pastries & Roses

Join us for brunch, teatime, and candlelit dinner reservations.

No time to sip your tea leisurely? Stop by for a pastry, grab some tea from our gift shop, or stop by and briskly walk through our rose garden.

Directions

123 Rose Lane, Portland, OR. From the downtown Voodoo Donuts, head east towards the waterfront and turn left on Rose Lane. We're the cottage with the iron wrought fence with rose emblems and stained glass windows.
