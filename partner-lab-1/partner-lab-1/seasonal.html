Seasonal and Regional Plants

Different plants like to be planted in different seasons and areas. Find out more below!

Planting Regionally

Any plant can't just be planted wherever. As mentioned on the home page, every area has a different "zone". These zones basically work to divide massive areas by temperature and water conditions. If you look up your county and state, you should easily be able to find your gardening zone. This dictates the best plants to grow in your local conditions! You of course don't have to plant by your zone, but it will prove to be more difficult.

Planting Seasonally

As you can guess by this point, not every plant is ideal for every season. Some like more sun or warmer temps, some like more shade or colder temps. The biggest determination of what you should plant (after determining your zone) is the amount of sunlight available in your conditions. To put it simply, different plants have different required amounts of sun. Some plants even prefer to have shade most of the day!


The season is another variable that can affect what you should grow. You may be be thinking of corn in summer or pumpkins in the fall. Below is a chart of some good plants to put in your small scale garden, but as always, the best determination is by your zone and available sunlight.
