About R&J

Est.2026
"Good music feels like a warm cup of tea on a cool autumn day."

R&J - Records and Jazz - is a cozy little record shop for people who love soft, dreamy jazz and rhythm and blues. We stock vinyls from modern jazz-pop favorites to timeless legends.

Come in, flip through the crates, and stay a while. Every record in our shop is picked by hand, and there is always something playing on the turntable.

We believe a good record is more than just music. It is a little piece of a mood you get to take home.

Let us know your favorite jazz artist and put in a request for an album, and get a free sticker!
